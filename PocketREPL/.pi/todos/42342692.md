## Completed

Implemented real llama.cpp C bindings in `LlamaBackend.swift`:

### LlamaContext Implementation
- ✅ Model loading with `llama_model_load_from_file()` and `llama_init_from_model()`
- ✅ Tokenization with `llama_tokenize()` and `llama_token_to_piece()`
- ✅ Inference loop with `llama_decode()` and `llama_batch_get_one()`
- ✅ Sampler chain with `llama_sampler_chain_init()`, `llama_sampler_init_*()`, `llama_sampler_sample()`
- ✅ Memory management with `llama_memory_clear()`, `llama_free()`, `llama_model_free()`
- ✅ Streaming generation with token-by-token callbacks
- ✅ Cancellation support with thread-safe stop flag
- ✅ Metal GPU support on iOS device (99 GPU layers)
- ✅ CPU-only mode for simulator
