Your pocket-sized JavaScript playground with an autonomous AI coding assistant — completely offline.

PocketREPL transforms your iPhone into a powerful coding environment. Give it a prompt, and watch as the AI writes JavaScript code, runs it in a sandboxed runtime, observes any errors, and iterates until it works. All of this happens locally on your device — no internet required.

HOW IT WORKS

• Type a natural language prompt describing what you want to build
• The AI generates JavaScript code and executes it instantly
• If something fails, it automatically debugs and retries
• Watch your code come to life in real-time

LOCAL AI MODELS

Choose from multiple Qwen coding models optimized for on-device inference:

• Qwen2.5-Coder-0.5B — Tiny but capable, fast inference
• Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B — Best balance of quality and speed
• Qwen2.5-Coder-3B — More capable for complex tasks
• Add your own custom GGUF models

DEVELOPER FEATURES

• Sandboxed JavaScriptCore runtime with CommonJS require() support
• File system workspace for organizing your projects
• Built-in console.log, assert, and test helpers
• Real-time execution with detailed error traces
• Context-aware AI that remembers your conversation

PRIVACY FIRST

Everything runs on your device. Your code, your prompts, your data — none of it leaves your iPhone. No accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry.
