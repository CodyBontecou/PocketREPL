A cutting-edge experiment in on-device AI coding — completely offline.

PocketREPL lets you explore what's possible when a local LLM writes and runs code directly on your iPhone. This is early, experimental technology with real limitations — think of it as a glimpse into the future of edge AI, not a production tool.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

This app runs local AI models with a 4096 token context window. That's roughly 3,000 words of memory — enough for small scripts, but not complex applications. The AI will forget earlier parts of long conversations, struggle with multi-file projects, and sometimes lose track of what it's building.

Real-world applications are not yet feasible. This is a sandbox for experimentation.

WHAT IT'S FOR

• Curiosity — see how far on-device AI has come
• Learning — watch an AI iterate through simple coding problems
• Fun — build small scripts and utilities with conversational prompts
• Exploration — test the boundaries of local LLMs on mobile hardware

HOW IT WORKS

• Type a natural language prompt
• The AI generates JavaScript and runs it instantly
• If it fails, it tries to debug and fix itself
• Everything happens locally — no internet, no cloud

LOCAL AI MODELS

Choose from Qwen coding models optimized for on-device inference:

• Qwen2.5-Coder-0.5B — Smallest and fastest
• Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B — Best balance for most prompts
• Qwen2.5-Coder-3B — More capable, slower inference
• Add your own custom GGUF models

Larger models are smarter but slower. Even the best local models can't match cloud AI — that's the tradeoff for privacy and offline capability.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Works well:
• Simple algorithms and utilities
• Math calculations and conversions
• Text processing scripts
• Learning JavaScript concepts
• One-off automation snippets

Struggles with:
• Complex multi-step projects
• Code that builds on earlier conversation context
• Anything requiring sustained "memory" of prior work
• Production-quality applications

PRIVACY FIRST

Everything runs on your device. Your code, your prompts, your data — none of it leaves your iPhone. No accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry.

WHO SHOULD DOWNLOAD THIS

• Developers curious about edge AI capabilities
• Anyone who wants to experiment with local LLMs
• People interested in the future of on-device AI
• Learners who want AI help with simple code

WHO SHOULD WAIT

• Anyone expecting production-ready AI coding
• Users who need complex, multi-file project support
• Those comparing this to ChatGPT or Claude

PocketREPL is a window into what's coming. The technology will improve. For now, enjoy exploring the frontier.
